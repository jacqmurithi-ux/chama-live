Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers about what CHAMA LIVE is, how groups get started, and what the platform currently supports.

What is CHAMA LIVE?

CHAMA LIVE is a digital management system for groups. It helps organise member records, contributions, expenses, meetings and reports in one place.

Who is CHAMA LIVE for?

It is designed for chamas and community groups that want to move away from scattered paper and spreadsheet records into a shared digital workspace.

What can group administrators manage?

The current platform includes group administration, member records, contribution records, expenses, meetings, reports and related group-management workflows.

Do members have their own access?

Yes. CHAMA LIVE has member-facing pages and a member login experience separate from the group administration workspace.

How much does the Chama plan cost?

The published Chama plan is KSh 500 per group per month, with the first month free. See the Pricing page for the current published options.

Does CHAMA LIVE support payments?

Payment-related capabilities are being developed and validated carefully. Available payment methods can depend on the group's configured setup, so CHAMA LIVE does not promise one universal payment rail for every group.

How do I create a group?

Start from Create Group, create the administrator account and follow the onboarding process presented by CHAMA LIVE.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Contact CHAMA LIVE support to discuss account or subscription changes. Any applicable billing terms are governed by the Terms of Service.

Where can I report a problem?

Contact us at mjtees35@gmail.com and include the group name and a clear description of the issue.
