How to use this demo

Everyone can explore the fictional E2600 group data. Use the Dashboard, Members and Meetings tabs to see how CHAMA LIVE brings group information together. The demo is intentionally open: it does not simulate member/admin restrictions.

In a real group: access and actions depend on the person's authorized role. Group officers handle protected management tasks such as managing members, creating/completing meetings and recording attendance, while ordinary members receive the information and actions available to their role. Financial and other sensitive operations are also authorization-controlled.
