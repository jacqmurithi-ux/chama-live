Group Admin: Set up your chama

For the person who creates the chama account through sign-up and becomes Group Admin / Member 001. This is not the Platform Admin. Work through these steps before using CHAMA LIVE for live group records.

Keep the roles separate: The Group Admin signs up and onboards their own group. The Platform Admin is a separate platform-level role. If platform authorization is required, only the implemented Platform Admin workflow can perform it; the group creator cannot approve their own group.

Status gap: Public copy describes email confirmation and platform review, but the current js/auth.js documentation says its authentication core has no Platform Admin review dependency. The expected Pending → Active journey must be verified in signup/status enforcement before claiming it works.

1. Group creation journey

  1. Open Create Group / Sign up. Enter accurate details with an email you can access.
  2. If Supabase sends a confirmation email, open it and follow the link. Check Spam/Junk. Whether confirmation is mandatory depends on deployed Auth settings.
  3. Submit the application as directed and keep any confirmation message.
  4. Sign in through Admin Portal. Supabase Auth checks credentials; CHAMA LIVE resolves your member/group context.
  5. If access is blocked, contact support. Do not change IDs or database records to get around it.

Email unconfirmed: behaviour depends on Auth configuration. Pending: public copy describes review, but reviewed auth documentation does not prove a Pending gate. Active: status alone does not prove every action is authorized. No group context: ask support to check the member relationship.

2. First login: eight checklist steps

The Admin Portal loads an onboarding checklist. Its current predicates are in js/onboarding-checklist.js. It deliberately leaves opening balances incomplete because no authoritative completion record is exposed. A tick is not a financial audit.

1. Group profile — Group Management

Review group name, category, registration details, phone, email, country and description. The checklist checks name, category, country and at least one phone/email. Correct and save; reopen to verify.

2. Officers assigned — Members / Group Management

Identify the correct members, then use the available officer assignment area. The checklist checks Chairperson, Treasurer and Secretary in member position fields. Do not create duplicates. Verify names and positions. Admin is not automatically an elected officer.

3. Members added — Members / Add Member

Review the roster and add only people not already listed. The checklist requires more than one member row. Enter fields requested by the form; confirm the saved member appears.

4. Contribution rules — Group Management

Agree amount, frequency, due date and contribution type, then save. The checklist checks a positive group monthly contribution or an active member contribution rule. Reopen and verify.

5. Modules — Group Settings

Review Loans and Merry-go-round, plus Plans & Activities, Assets, Milestones and Data Migration. The settings page says the four newer modules default off. Enable only what the group needs and save. The checklist checks that module metadata exists; it does not require a module to be on. Disabling a module blocks access without deleting saved data; it does not grant approval rights.

6. Opening balances — supported workflow only

Finish members and rules first. Reconcile the old cashbook and agree one cut-off date (the date through which old records have been checked). Use only the opening-balance feature actually available; verify member, amount, date and source records. Never edit database rows or record old payments as new money.

  • Members first: each figure must attach to the right person.
  • Rules first: the balance must be interpreted against the correct obligations.
  • Cut-off date: separates old books from new activity and helps prevent double counting or false arrears.
  • Mistakes include wrong member, inconsistent dates, mixing opening figures with new contributions or using unverified amounts. Exact UI/RPC and checklist rule remain gaps.

7. Constitution — Documents

Use the available upload control; choose Constitution category if offered and select the approved file. The checklist looks for document category/type containing “constitution”. Confirm it appears and opens. Do not assume a phone file uploaded.

8. First meeting — Meetings

Enter agreed date, time, venue/details and purpose requested by the form. The checklist looks for a meeting dated today or later whose status is not cancelled/completed. Save and confirm it appears; do not mark it complete before it happens.

  2. The app resolves user → member → group. Do not trust group IDs from URLs/forms.

5. Day 1 to first live month

Day 1

  • ☐ Confirm email and sign in with your own account.
  • ☐ Verify group name and contacts.
  • ☐ Confirm Admin and elected officers.
  • ☐ Agree the member roster and correct duplicates.

Before first contributions

  • ☐ Save agreed rules/due dates.
  • ☐ Choose modules the group will use.
  • ☐ Upload approved constitution and essential documents.
  • ☐ Schedule first meeting.
  • ☐ Reconcile old records to one cut-off date.
  • ☐ Enter opening balances through supported workflow and verify totals.

First live month

  • ☐ Verify contributions against reliable evidence.
  • ☐ Use supported contribution workflow; never write ledger rows directly.
  • ☐ Review arrears/credit with Treasurer and reconcile discrepancies.
  • ☐ Record expenses and meeting attendance.
  • ☐ Review reports and close only after responsible officers check figures.
  • ☐ Record unresolved issues and ask support.

Troubleshooting

  • Email not confirmed: Check address and Spam/Junk; use current link or supported resend/support route.
  • Pending group: Ask support to verify status/authorization. Do not change status data.
  • Member cannot log in: Check portal, login identifier, password, confirmation and member-to-group link. Never share credentials.
  • Wrong officer: Correct through supported officer/Group Management area, save and verify; otherwise contact support.
  • Opening balance error: Stop posting; check member, source ledger, amount and cut-off with Treasurer and use approved correction.
  • Checklist incomplete: Verify saved data, refresh, then report the step if still incomplete.

Gaps found

  1. Pending → Active: public copy describes review, but js/auth.js says auth core has no platform-admin review dependency. Verify signup RPC, status fields, Platform Admin tools and gates. The Group Admin who signs up is not the Platform Admin.
  2. Email confirmation setting and resend/recovery path are not established by reviewed frontend files.
  3. The checklist deliberately leaves opening balances incomplete. Other predicates are simplified indicators (member count, officer position fields, module metadata, constitution metadata and future meeting date/status); verify they match group practice.
  4. Verify opening-balance UI/RPC, cut-off handling, correction path and checklist predicate.
  5. Review RLS/RPC and test every role/action; frontend allowlist alone is not proof.
  6. Verify handover/member deactivation and historical-record behaviour.
  7. Confirm the deployed Admin navigation label is clear.

Quick recap

  • Use your own confirmed account; never share passwords.
  • Check group, officers and members before contribution rules.
  • Opening balances follow the roster/rules and one cut-off date.
  • Upload the approved constitution and schedule the first meeting.
  • Admin differs from elected officers and Platform Admin.
  • Front-end checks and database rules both matter.
  • If status, permissions or balances look wrong, stop and ask support.

Quiz: 10 questions with answers

1. Who is Member 001?

The first group administrator; not automatically an elected officer.

2. Does Pending always become Active after approval?

Not proven by reviewed auth code; support must verify the workflow.

3. What if email confirmation fails?

Check address, Spam/Junk, current link and supported resend/support route.

4. Why enter members before balances?

So each figure attaches to the correct member.

5. Why a cut-off date?

It separates old records from new activity and prevents double counting/false arrears.

6. Can Admin automatically approve a loan?

No; documented approval requires Chairperson, Treasurer and Secretary.

7. What proves a member was added?

Successful save and visible roster entry.

8. Is hiding a button enough for security?

No; RLS and protected database functions must enforce access.

9. What if an opening balance is wrong?

Stop posting, check source/member/amount/date, and use approved correction.

10. Can you give the incoming Admin your password?

No; use the supported role process and keep credentials private.
