Before closing the month

  • Confirm all contributions for the selected month have been recorded.
  • Confirm all expenses have the correct approval status.
  • Only approved expenses are deducted from the closing balance.
  • Pending and rejected expenses do not reduce the closing balance.
  • Once a period is closed, its stored closing balance becomes the opening balance reference for the next period.
  • Review the financial position carefully before confirming the closing.
