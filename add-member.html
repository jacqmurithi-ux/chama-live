How member login works

  1. Admin or Chairperson adds the member.
  2. The member record is created with Pending status.
  3. The member uses their membership number to activate their account.
  4. They create their email/password login.
  5. CHAMA LIVE links their Auth account to their existing member record.
  6. Their predefined role controls the dashboard they can access.
