Your Workspace

Your workspace contains the tools used to manage your chama or group.

  • Dashboard — see your group's key information.
  • Members — manage member records and accounts.
  • Contributions — record and review contributions.
  • Expenses — manage group expenses.
  • Meetings — keep track of meetings and resolutions.
  • Reports — review group information and activity.
  • Group Management — manage group-level settings.
✓

Your First Steps

If your group is new to CHAMA LIVE, start with these basic steps.

  • Confirm your group information.
  • Add or review your members.
  • Confirm member roles and account status.
  • Start recording contributions.
  • Record expenses and meetings as they happen.
  • Use reports to review your group's activity.

Common Questions

Use these quick answers while learning the system.

  • Where do I begin? Start with the Guided Tour.
  • Where are members managed? Open Members from the main navigation.
  • Where do I record money received? Use Contributions.
  • Where do I record spending? Use Expenses.
  • Where do I manage group settings? Open Group Management.
