Privacy Policy

How CHAMA LIVE handles information used to provide the service. Last updated: September 2026.

This policy is intentionally limited to information and processing relevant to the CHAMA LIVE platform. It does not claim payment, analytics, KYC, SMS or other integrations that are not actually enabled for a particular account.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how the service is used, CHAMA LIVE may process account details such as name, email address and phone number; group information; member records; contribution, expense, meeting and reporting records; authentication and security information; and technical information needed to operate the website.

2. Information entered by groups

Group administrators are responsible for ensuring they are authorised to enter member information and other records. Groups should avoid uploading unnecessary personal information and should use the platform according to their own legal and governance obligations.

3. How information is used

Information is used to create and manage accounts, provide group-management features, display records to authorised users, support authentication, maintain security, troubleshoot issues, improve the service and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Group-level access

CHAMA LIVE is designed around group-scoped records. Access to information is controlled through the application's authentication and authorisation mechanisms. Users should not share credentials or attempt to access another group's information.

5. Payment information

Payment integrations are being developed and validated separately. CHAMA LIVE does not ask users to provide an M-Pesa PIN to the platform. Where an external payment provider is used, information may also be processed under that provider's own privacy terms.

6. Service providers

We may use infrastructure and service providers necessary to host, secure and operate CHAMA LIVE. Information is shared only as reasonably necessary for those services, legal obligations, security, or the operation of a feature the group has chosen to use.

7. Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain appropriate business and financial records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Specific retention periods may depend on the type of record and the group's obligations.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to processing. Requests can be made through the contact address below. Where a group controls records about its members, CHAMA LIVE may need to involve the relevant group administrator in responding to a request.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. If you identify a security concern, please report it promptly rather than attempting to exploit it.

10. Children

CHAMA LIVE is intended for group and organisational use. Groups are responsible for ensuring their use of member information complies with applicable rules concerning children and other protected information.

11. Policy changes

We may update this policy as CHAMA LIVE develops. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new update date.

12. Contact

Privacy questions or requests can be sent to mjtees35@gmail.com.
